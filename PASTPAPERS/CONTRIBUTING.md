# Contributing Past Papers

You don't need to know how to code, use git, or install anything. Pick whichever way is easier for you:

## Option 1: Google Form (quickest, no account needed)

**[Submit via Google Form →](https://forms.gle/AXXHReuKv9voDzXC9)**

Upload your paper directly, no GitHub account required. It'll be added to the repo by a maintainer shortly after.

## Option 2: Upload directly on GitHub

Prefer to see it go straight into the repo yourself? Everything happens in your browser, no git knowledge required.

1. **Go to the [pastpapers folder](https://github.com/aleesha-10/hummingraph/tree/main/pastpapers)** on GitHub.
2. **Find (or create) your university's folder.** If your university doesn't have a folder yet:
   - Click **"Add file" → "Create new file"**
   - Type your university's name followed by a `/` and any filename, e.g. `FAST/readme.md` — typing the `/` automatically creates the folder for you.
3. **Upload your papers:**
   - Open your university's folder
   - Click **"Add file" → "Upload files"**
   - Drag and drop your PDFs (or click to browse your computer)
4. **Scroll down and click "Propose changes."** GitHub will automatically create a copy of the repo under your account and prepare your submission — you don't need to understand what this means, just click through.
5. **Click "Create pull request"** on the next page. That's it — your submission is in.

## What happens next

A maintainer will review your submission and merge it in, usually within a few days. You'll get a notification if anything needs fixing (e.g. wrong folder, unreadable scan, wrong file type).

## Guidelines

- **File format:** PDF preferred. Clear photos/scans are fine if that's all you have.
- **Naming:** something recognizable helps everyone, e.g. `CS201-Midterm-Fall2023.pdf` rather than `scan001.pdf`.
- **Folder structure:** `pastpapers/<university>/<course-or-subject>/`. If you're unsure where something goes, just upload it anywhere sensible — a maintainer can move it.
- **Only upload past papers here.** Anything else (typos, feature requests, bugs) should go through [Issues](https://github.com/aleesha-10/hummingraph/issues) instead.

## Questions?

Open an [issue](https://github.com/aleesha-10/hummingraph/issues) and ask — no submission is too small.
